How to Create Custom QR Codes Step-by-Step

  1. Select Category Type: Choose between Website Links, WhatsApp direct messaging, Wi-Fi credentials, Contact Cards (vCard), Geolocation, Email, SMS, or Plain Text.
  2. Enter Target Details: Fill in your URL, phone number, or network password in Step 2. All encoding happens client-side directly in your browser.
  3. Customize & Brand: Upload a custom brand logo or select a preset icon. Select custom color themes, dot patterns, and outer eye shapes.
  4. Download Instantly: Export your finalized QR code as a high-resolution PNG image or scalable Vector SVG file suitable for commercial print.

Why Use Vector SVG Format for Printing?

When printing QR codes on business cards, brochures, banners, or product packaging, raster images like PNG can become pixelated when scaled up. SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) retains perfect sharpness at any resolution, ensuring high scan reliability across all mobile camera devices.
